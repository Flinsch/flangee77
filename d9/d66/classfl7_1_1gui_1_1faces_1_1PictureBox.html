Detailed Description

A display-only image, shown per get_image_scale_mode() (default: Stretch; see ImageScaleMode): distorted to exactly fill this face's own bounds, uniformly scaled (preserving its own aspect ratio) to fit within them, or drawn at its own native size, centered. Also HasBackground, so a themed fill/chrome shows through wherever the image itself doesn't fully cover this face's own bounds (Zoom's/Center's letterboxing), same as any other boxed face's.
