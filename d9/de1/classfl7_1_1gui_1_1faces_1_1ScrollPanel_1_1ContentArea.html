Detailed Description

The actual (arbitrary, publicly addable) content viewport: a plain Container except its children are shifted by the owning ScrollPanel's current scroll position (see Collection::get_scroll_offset()). The mutator is deliberately not public: scrolling an owning ScrollPanel must always go through its own set_scroll_position() instead, which clamps the position and keeps both scroll bars in sync with it – setting it here directly would desync them.
