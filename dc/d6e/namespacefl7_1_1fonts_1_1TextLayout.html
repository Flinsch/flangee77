Splits the given code points into lines.

Explicit line breaks (CRLF, LF, or CR) always start a new line, regardless of the wrap mode. If text_style.wrap_mode is WrapMode::Word and max_width is positive, each such paragraph is additionally word-wrapped to fit within max_width (greedily, breaking at whitespace; a single word wider than max_width is hard-broken mid-word). Leading/trailing whitespace of each produced line is excluded from its range and width. text_style.letter_spacing and text_style.word_spacing are incorporated into each line's measured width and into wrap decisions.
