Detailed Description

A single selectable row, purely a disabled decoration (themed "list_box_row", see HasCheckedState) around its own caller-supplied content Face (see set_content()): never itself hit-tested, hovered, pressed, or focused (that's RowArea's job, for the whole list at once; see ListBox::RowArea), only visually reflects whether it's currently the owning list's selected item.
