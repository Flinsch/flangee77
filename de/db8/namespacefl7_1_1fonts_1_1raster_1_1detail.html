std::vector< ml7::QuadraticBezier2f > fl7::fonts::raster::detail::transform_glyph_segments ( const Glyph &  glyph,
float  font_size 
)

Transforms all of the glyph's contour segments from font-normalized (em, y-up) space into pixel space (scaled by font_size, y-down), flattening them into a single list of quadratic Bezier curves. Contour boundaries are not preserved; use transform_glyph_contours if per-contour grouping is required.
