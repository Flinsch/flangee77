Detailed Description

Arranges a container's children one after another along a single axis (see Orientation), separated by a fixed get_gap(), within a fixed get_padding() kept around the whole sequence. This divides the container into a sequence of cells, one per child: along the main axis, a cell is either exactly as big as its own child's get_preferred_size() (a stretch factor of 0 or less; see get_stretch()) or a share of whatever space is left over once every such fixed-size child's cell has been accounted for, proportional to its own stretch factor relative to the other stretched children's (every child's default: see get_stretch(), every cell then just splits the main axis evenly, unless some are explicitly pinned to their own preferred size via 0 instead); along the perpendicular (cross) axis, every cell is simply the container's own full extent there (minus padding).

How a child actually sits within its own (possibly larger than its own preferred size) cell – filled, shrunk-and-aligned near/far, or shrunk-and-centered – is then up to its own Face::get_anchor() (the same flags DefaultLayout reads for the other purpose of reacting to a resize), independently per axis: Left/Top alone (whichever applies to that axis; the default anchor) aligns it near, at its own get_preferred_size(); Right/Bottom alone aligns it far instead, same size; neither aligns it centered; both together fills the cell, overriding its own preferred size. A stretched child (the default; see get_stretch()) whose anchor doesn't fill the main axis this way just sits somewhere within its own, larger cell instead of actually growing – stretch and anchor are independent, exactly as they already are for the cross axis. A plain stack of fixed-size children, each kept at its own preferred size regardless of this layout's own size, therefore needs 0 explicitly set on every one of them (see set_stretch()), not just left at the default.

Each child's own get_preferred_size() is captured once, the first time perform() ever sees it, and reused from then on, rather than re-queried live on every call: get_preferred_size()'s own default is simply get_size() (see Face), so re-querying it live would just be asking this layout what size it itself last set that child to, e.g., a child shrunk once (cross axis smaller than its preferred size; see below) could then never grow back, since its "preferred" size would already have become whatever it was just shrunk to. This holds only as long as nothing overrides get_preferred_size() to be genuinely dynamic; revisit (e.g., an explicit cache-invalidation hook) once something does.

◆ set_stretch()

void fl7::gui::layout::BoxLayout::set_stretch ( const Face & child,
float stretch )

Sets the given child's stretch factor: 0 or less means its cell is exactly its own get_preferred_size() along this layout's own axis, not participating in the distribution at all; a positive value (1, the default for every child until told otherwise) means its cell instead takes that share of whatever space is left over once every non-stretched (stretch <= 0) child's cell has taken its own preferred size, relative to the other stretched children's own factors (e.g., two children both left at the default 1 split the leftover space evenly; one set to 2 and another left at 1 split it two-to-one). Either way, purely about the cell's own size, independent of whether the child actually fills it (see this class's own doc comment: that's its anchor's call, not this one's).
