Detailed Description

A plain, inert handle for a Signal subscription: does NOT disconnect on its own, neither on destruction nor when overwritten (e.g., via move-assignment). Signal::connect() returns one of these, and discarding it needs no ceremony at all (the common case: "stay connected for the `Signal`'s lifetime"). Use disconnect() to sever the subscription early, or wrap this in a ScopedConnection for the less common case of a lifetime-bound subscription.

Safe to outlive the Signal it was connected to: disconnecting then is a no-op.
