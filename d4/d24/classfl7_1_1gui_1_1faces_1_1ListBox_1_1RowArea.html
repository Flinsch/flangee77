Detailed Description

Spans every row at once (see ListBox::_relayout_rows()): the one face that's actually hit-tested/focused for the whole list, so a click anywhere in a row (its disabled content Face included) selects that row, and arrow-key navigation only needs this single face to hold the focus, rather than each (otherwise disabled) row needing to be its own focusable Control.

ArrowResponse fl7::gui::faces::ListBox::RowArea::_consumes_arrow ( ArrowDirection dir) const
overrideprotectedvirtual

Consumes Up/Down (hard) while the selection still has somewhere to go in that direction (from no selection, either arrow enters the list), so plain arrow-key focus navigation yields to _on_key_down() moving the selection instead; yields back once at either end. The horizontal arrows always stay free.
