Detailed Description

A face that owns children: adds child ownership and the recursive hit-test traversal on top of face's leaf behavior. Children are exposed read-only for external tree traversal (e.g., by a renderer), but a collection itself knows nothing about drawing/rendering. Child ownership stops here deliberately. See Container (arbitrary, publicly addable children) and Compound (fixed, internally managed children) for the two ways of actually exposing this.
