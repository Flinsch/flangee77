Detailed Description

A bare top-level window: a background/border chrome (see Style) around an internal content area, exposed via get_content_area() rather than a direct add_child() on the Window itself (Window is a Compound: fixed internal composition, not arbitrary public children). The content area fills the entire window and is kept in sync as the window is resized. Chrome beyond the plain background (title bar, close button, resize handles, ...) is left to a future Frame built on top of this.
