◆ text_style

fonts::TextStyle fl7::gui::Style::text_style = _default_text_style()

A Face's text is always drawn in box mode (a Face always has a fixed position/size), so, unlike fonts::TextStyle's own generic default (Baseline, meant for the point-anchored draw_text API), it defaults to Middle here.
