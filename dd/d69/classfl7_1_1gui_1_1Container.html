Detailed Description

A Collection with arbitrary, publicly addable children (e.g. Panel, GroupBox). Intended to eventually position its children via a pluggable layout (see the planned gui::layout namespace: DefaultLayout/BoxLayout/ GridLayout) – not implemented yet, children just keep whatever position/ size they're given for now.
