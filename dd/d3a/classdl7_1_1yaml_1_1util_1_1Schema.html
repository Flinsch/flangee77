Detailed Description

Resolves plain (unquoted) scalars according to the YAML 1.2 core schema, which is what decides whether null, true, 0x1f, or .inf is a value in its own right or just a string.

Reader and writer are two sides of the same rule and therefore share this: the reader resolves what it reads, and the writer asks whether a string would survive being written without quotes at all, which is the case exactly when the string resolves back to itself.
