enum struct fl7::gui::Anchor

Which of this face's own parent-relative edges stay anchored as its parent is resized (see layout::DefaultLayout), or, reusing the same flags for the perpendicular axis, how it's aligned within the space it's given along that axis (see layout::BoxLayout). Combinable (see operator|). Meaningless on its own: only consulted by a layout actually attached to this face's parent (see Container::set_layout()); with none attached, this face keeps whatever position/size it's explicitly given, regardless of its own anchor.

◆ ArrowResponse

enum struct fl7::gui::ArrowResponse
strong

How a focused face responds to an arrow in a given direction, i.e., whether spatial focus navigation (see Shell::focus_toward()) should yield to the face handling that arrow itself instead of moving focus.

Enumerator
Ignore 

The arrow is free for focus navigation.

Soft 

The face uses it (e.g., a text caret), but forced navigation (gamepad, Alt+arrow) may override.

Hard 

The face needs it (e.g., an open list with somewhere left to go) even under forced navigation.

◆ ImageScaleMode

enum struct fl7::gui::ImageScaleMode
strong

How a HasImage face's image (see HasImage::get_image()) is fit into its own bounds.

Enumerator
Stretch 

Stretches the image to exactly fill the face's own bounds, distorting its aspect ratio if necessary.

Zoom 

Scales the image uniformly (preserving its own aspect ratio) to fit entirely within the face's own bounds, centered; letterboxed on whichever axis has room left over.

Center 

Draws the image at its own native size, centered: clipped (see AbstractRenderer::_draw_face()) if larger than the face's own bounds, letterboxed if smaller.

◆ ScrollBarVisibility

enum struct fl7::gui::ScrollBarVisibility
strong

A scroll bar's visibility, independent of whether its own axis (or, for a single-axis scroller like faces::TextField, the only axis there is) is actually scrollable/overflowing at all. Shared by every face that offers this choice (see faces::ScrollPanel, faces::TextField), rather than each redeclaring its own, identical enum.

Enumerator
Always 

Shows the scroll bar unconditionally (even while its own axis doesn't actually overflow, in which case it just sits there inert, full-range, nothing to drag).

Never 

Hides the scroll bar unconditionally.

Auto 

Shows the scroll bar only while the content actually overflows that axis's (viewport) bounds.
