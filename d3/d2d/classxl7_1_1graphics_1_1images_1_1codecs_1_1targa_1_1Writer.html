enum struct xl7::graphics::images::codecs::targa::Writer::Origin
strong

Defines the image origin location (coordinate 0,0) used in the output file.

Enumerator
BottomLeft 

The origin is at the bottom-left corner of the image (i.e., scanlines are stored bottom-to-top). This is the traditional TGA convention used by many legacy tools.

TopLeft 

The origin is at the top-left corner of the image (i.e., scanlines are stored top-to-bottom). This corresponds to the layout used by most modern image formats and graphics frameworks (including this one).
