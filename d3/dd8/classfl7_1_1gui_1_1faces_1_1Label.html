Detailed Description

A display-only piece of text, single-line or multi-line, laid out within this face's bounds. No wrapping/alignment logic of its own, that's entirely up to the renderer's text-drawing backend (see AbstractRenderer::draw_text_in_box).

◆ _calculate_text_size()

ml7::Vector2f fl7::gui::faces::Label::_calculate_text_size ( ) const
private

Returns this label's text's own natural (unwrapped) size: however wide its single widest (explicit-break-delimited) line is, times however many such lines there are, under its effective font/text style. {0, 0} if it currently has no effective font (e.g., not yet part of a themed Shell). Cached, as long as neither the text nor the effective font have changed since.
