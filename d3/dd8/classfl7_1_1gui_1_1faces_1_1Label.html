cl7::u32string _text
 

Detailed Description

A display-only piece of text, single-line or multi-line, laid out within this face's bounds. No wrapping/alignment logic of its own, that's entirely up to the renderer's text-drawing backend (see AbstractRenderer::draw_text_in_box).
