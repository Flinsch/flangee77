Detailed Description

An editable piece of text: click to position the caret, type to insert at it, move it (Left/Right/Home/End, or word-at-a-time with Ctrl+Left/Right), or delete around it (Backspace/Delete). Also supports selecting a range, either by dragging the mouse, by holding Shift while moving the caret (word-at-a-time too, combined with Ctrl), or all at once with Ctrl+A. A selection then gets replaced/deleted as a whole by typing/Backspace/Delete, same as it would in any other text editor, or copied/cut/pasted via the system clipboard with Ctrl+C/X/V.

Single-line by default: no wrapping/scrolling, text that overflows the field's width is simply clipped (see AbstractRenderer::_draw_face()), and Enter does nothing (consumed by Shell for focus activation instead, see Shell::update()). set_multiline_enabled(true) opts into word-wrapping and literal Enter/line-break support, plus Up/Down moving the caret a line at a time and Home/End meaning the current LINE's start/end rather than the whole text's (Ctrl+Home/Ctrl+End still jump to the very start/end regardless), plus a vertical scroll bar of its own (see Compound; a fixed composition, same as ScrollBar's own track + buttons), shown, by default, only while the text actually overflows this field's own height (see gui::ScrollBarVisibility/set_scroll_bar_visibility()), that keeps the caret scrolled into view automatically as it moves (see _ensure_caret_visible()), and that the mouse wheel also scrolls while hovered. Note: the scroll bar being its own genuine Tab stop (same as, e.g., faces::ScrollPanel's own scroll bars) means it, not just this field itself, can end up focused.

void fl7::gui::faces::TextField::_adjust_effective_text_style ( fonts::TextStyle & text_style) const
overrideprotectedvirtual

An editable field must always show exactly what was typed, so preserve_whitespace is non-negotiable, not theme-driven; wrap_mode likewise always follows get_multiline_enabled() directly, regardless of whatever a theme happens to set, so rendering always matches this field's own caret/selection/hit-testing math (see _lay_out()). While actually scrolling (the scroll bar is shown; see _relayout()), vertical_align is additionally pinned to Top: Middle/Bottom would otherwise position the (now overflowing) text block so that even its very first line starts above this field's own top edge at scroll position 0, which isn't what "scrolled all the way up" should look like. Left alone (not actually overflowing yet), whatever the theme/ override sets is honored as-is, same as a single-line field's.

void fl7::gui::faces::TextField::_on_key_down ( xl7::input::Key key)
overrideprotectedvirtual

Moves the caret, or deletes the code point before/after it (or, with an active selection, deletes the whole selection instead). Ctrl+Left/Right moves a whole word at a time instead of one code point. Shift+Left/Right/ Home/End/Ctrl+Left/Ctrl+Right extends the selection instead of moving/ collapsing it; Ctrl+A selects everything outright. Ctrl+C/X/V copies/cuts/ pastes the selection via the system clipboard (see xl7::Clipboard). While get_multiline_enabled(): Up/Down move the caret a line at a time (keeping roughly the same column); Home/End jump to the current LINE's start/end rather than the whole text's (Ctrl+Home/Ctrl+End still do, regardless). Scrolls the caret into view afterward, if necessary.

◆ _relayout()

void fl7::gui::faces::TextField::_relayout ( )
private

Re-decides (while get_multiline_enabled() and there's a font to lay out against) whether the scroll bar is actually shown, per get_scroll_bar_visibility(): Always unconditionally; Auto only once this field's content actually overflows its own height at its full width, or, if not yet, once more at the width actually left over once the scroll bar takes SCROLLBAR_THICKNESS off of it (a narrower width only ever wraps to more, never fewer, lines, so this can't flip back off and one re-check is enough to converge, same reasoning as faces::ScrollPanel's own single-axis visibility resolution); Never, well, never. Either way, sizes/ positions the scroll bar accordingly (always, even hidden, so it's ready to reappear at the right place/size), and updates its range/ visible_fraction/step (one line) from the result, re-clamping the current scroll position to it (see _set_scroll_offset()). While !get_multiline_enabled() or there's no font yet, simply hides/disables the scroll bar and resets the scroll position to 0 instead, regardless of get_scroll_bar_visibility().
