Detailed Description

A togglable box with a persistent checked/unchecked value (see HasCheckedState) and an optional caption to its right. Internally a small fixed composition (see Compound): a passive "box" glyph (see Box) sized to this face's own height and a Label filling the remaining width for the caption. Both are disabled (see Face::set_enabled), so a click or hover anywhere across box+caption always resolves to this CheckBox as a whole, never to either part individually.
