Detailed Description

A rasterizer that generates a multi-channel signed distance field (MSDF) for a glyph's outline. Compared to a plain (single-channel) SDF, corners are preserved by distributing the contour's edges across the color channels ("edge coloring") and reconstructing sharp corners via a per-pixel median of the three channels in the pixel shader, instead of the single distance value degrading corners into rounded blobs at large scale-up factors.
