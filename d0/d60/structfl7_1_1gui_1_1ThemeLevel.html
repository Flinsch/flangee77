Detailed Description

One cascading level of a theme: either the theme-wide default level, or a single face-type/role level. Inherits ThemeOverlay's own fields (chrome, font, text style, colors, padding, etc.), plus its own independent, not mutually exclusive checked/hovered/pressed/focused overlays, each a plain ThemeOverlay. See Theme::resolve() for how a level's fields cascade and its overlays combine.
