◆ GameControllerButton

enum struct xl7::input::GameControllerButton
strong

Identifies a game controller button in a backend-agnostic way. Mirrors the XInput button set, which is generic enough to also cover most other gamepad-like controllers (A/B/X/Y correspond to the bottom face-button layout of a standard Xbox-style pad).

◆ DEFAULT_CLICK_MOVE_THRESHOLD_PX

constexpr int xl7::input::DEFAULT_CLICK_MOVE_THRESHOLD_PX = 4
inlineconstexpr

The default maximum distance (in pixels) the mouse may move between a button's down and up transitions for the cycle to still count as a "click" (as opposed to a drag). Callers may override this per query.
