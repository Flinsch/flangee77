Detailed Description

Matches a TOML multi-line string delimited by three quote characters ("""...""" for basic strings or ‘’''...'''` for literal strings). Basic multi-line strings support backslash escapes (including line-ending backslashes); literal ones do not. A closing delimiter may be immediately preceded by up to two additional quote characters that are part of the content.
