Detailed Description

Keeps each child anchored to whichever of its own parent-relative edges its Face::get_anchor() flags (see gui::Anchor) say, as the container is resized: Right without Left keeps its distance from the right edge (shifting as the container grows/shrinks); Left and Right together instead stretches it to keep its distance from both; same for Top and Bottom along the vertical axis, independently. Left (or Top) alone, or no anchor at all, does nothing on either axis, same as having no layout at all, which is also the default (see Face::get_anchor()).

Each child's own "distance from its anchored edge(s)" is captured once, from its own position/size relative to the container's size as of the first perform() call that sees it (typically right when it's added), and kept fixed from then on; explicitly repositioning/resizing it afterwards is overwritten again on this layout's next perform() call, same as any other anchor-based GUI layout.
