Detailed Description

A plain background/border chrome (see Style) around arbitrary, publicly addable children: the Container-based counterpart to Window's Compound-based fixed content area. Purely a marker beyond that: no new members, just a distinct type so a renderer can tell "this container also wants a background drawn" from a bare Container that doesn't.
