Detailed Description

Abstract base class for texture-atlas-based text rendering.

Glyphs are rasterized on demand and cached in per-font-size texture atlases. During a batch, quads are accumulated and submitted together on flush. Concrete subclasses provide the rendering shader via _get_shader_path.
